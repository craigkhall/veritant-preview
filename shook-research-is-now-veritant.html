SHOOK Research is now Veritant.

SHOOK Research today announced that it is now Veritant Research. The new name reflects the independent, research-driven process that has guided the firm’s advisor rankings for more than a decade.

Veritant will continue to produce the rankings advisors and investors know, built on the same methodology: quantitative analysis paired with in-person due diligence. Over 10 years, the team has reviewed more than 50,000 survey submissions and met with over 7,000 advisors in person.

[Placeholder quote from Molly Bennard, Chief Executive Officer.]

Forbes remains Veritant’s strategic marketing partner, distributing and marketing the rankings. All previously published rankings remain live on Forbes.com.

The first rankings published under the Veritant name will be released in December 2026, starting with the Top RIA Firms.
