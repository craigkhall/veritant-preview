Our Commitment

Independent at every stage.

Veritant alone designs the methodology, evaluates the data, conducts due diligence, and determines the rankings. Advisors, firms, sponsors, and commercial partners have no influence over those decisions.

Qualification Criteria

Who can be considered.

Any advisor, team, or firm that meets a ranking’s minimum eligibility criteria is invited to apply, regardless of size, business model, or affiliation. A completed application is required for consideration.

Eligibility varies by list. Every applicant is evaluated against the same published criteria for that ranking.

There is no cost to apply. No advisor, firm, or other party can pay for placement.

Scoring Integrity

Clear criteria. Protected scoring.

We share what we measure and how we verify it, while keeping our exact scoring formula confidential to protect the integrity of the process. We continually review our methodology and publish meaningful updates here.
